Mutsumi

I'm only two years old,and I want to eat a hamburger. But Mom won't let me—she says hamburgers are unhealthy. But I don't think that's right—a hamburger is obviously the happiest thing in the world. It has bread, it has meat, and it has yummy cheese. It lies all alone in its box every day, waiting for me to save it. If I don't go and eat it, it will definitely be very sad.
